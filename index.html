About these results

Nearby Eats uses OpenStreetMap data through public Overpass API servers. Results are sorted using the straight-line distance from your device location.

Open Now is evaluated from each place's OpenStreetMap opening_hours data. Places without mapped hours are excluded when that filter is on.

Google Maps and Apple Maps buttons open the matching business in those services. Google ratings, reviews, photos, and other Google-specific details stay on Google Maps rather than being copied into Nearby Eats.

OpenStreetMap is community-maintained, so some businesses or details may be missing or out of date.

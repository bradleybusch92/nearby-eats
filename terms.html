Terms

Nearby Eats is a free convenience tool for finding nearby food. Business names, locations, hours, cuisine, and service details come from community-maintained OpenStreetMap data and may be incomplete, missing, or out of date.

The app relies on free public Overpass API infrastructure. Availability is not guaranteed and occasional temporary search failures can occur.

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.
